About SUS

Building a smarter, fairer, and more transparent fuel subsidy ecosystem for Malaysia.

Who We Are

SUS Company is a Malaysian technology firm dedicated to building smart, transparent, and accountable solutions for the nation's fuel subsidy ecosystem.

We specialize in OBD-RFID integration, vehicle verification technology, and real-time data analytics for government and commercial fleet applications.

Founded with a mission to eliminate fuel subsidy leakage, we combine hardware innovation with intelligent software to create a system that ensures every subsidized liter reaches its intended vehicle—and every intended vehicle only.

Our Solution

OBD-FuelGuard is a smart, tamper-proof fuel subsidy verification system that connects vehicles, petrol stations, and subsidy records.

OBD-RFID Hardware

Plug-and-play device that reads VIN and engine data in real-time. Tamper-proof design prevents unauthorized removal or modification.

VIN Verification

Real-time eligibility checks at the pump. Only verified vehicles on the subsidy list can access subsidized fuel.

Analytics Dashboard

Track fuel consumption, transaction history, and vehicle performance. Generate audit-ready reports for government reconciliation.
