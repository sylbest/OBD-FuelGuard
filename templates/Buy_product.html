Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before purchasing.

What is included in the hardware?

The OBD-FuelGuard device includes OBD-II connector, RFID reader, 4G/LTE module, and tamper-proof casing. Installation is plug-and-play.

Do I need to replace my fuel pumps?

No. OBD-FuelGuard integrates with your existing petrol station POS system via API. No hardware changes required at the pump.

Is there a monthly subscription fee?

Yes. The software platform requires a subscription starting from RM 49/month per vehicle. Bulk discounts are available for fleets.

How long is the warranty?

All OBD-FuelGuard hardware comes with a 1-year warranty. Professional and Enterprise packages include extended 2-year warranty.

Can I request a demo before purchasing?

Absolutely! We offer free demonstrations for government agencies and fleet operators with 50+ vehicles.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept bank transfers, credit cards, and government procurement orders. Enterprise clients can request invoice-based billing.
