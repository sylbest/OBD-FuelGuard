Frequently Asked Questions

How does OBD-FuelGuard prevent subsidy leakage?

OBD-FuelGuard uses OBD-RFID technology to verify a vehicle's identity at the point of fueling. The system cross-references the vehicle's OBD data with the subsidy database in real-time. Only verified, eligible vehicles can access subsidized fuel, and every transaction is logged immutably.

Is the system compatible with all petrol stations?

Yes. OBD-FuelGuard is designed for universal station coverage. It integrates with existing POS systems and requires minimal hardware installation. We support all major petrol station brands across Malaysia.

How long does installation take?

Vehicle OBD device installation takes less than 5 minutes per vehicle. Station-side integration typically takes 1-2 days per location, depending on existing infrastructure.

What data does OBD-FuelGuard collect?

We collect only what's necessary for verification: vehicle identification, fuel transaction data, and timestamps. All data is encrypted and compliant with Malaysia's PDPA regulations. We never collect personal driving data.

What is the pricing model?

Pricing is subscription-based and scales with your fleet size or number of stations. First-month adopters receive 50% off the subscription fee. Contact us for a customized quote.

Can it integrate with our existing fleet management system?

Absolutely. OBD-FuelGuard offers REST APIs and webhook integrations for popular fleet management platforms. Our team will work with you on custom integration if needed.
